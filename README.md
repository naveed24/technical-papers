## Q3: What are active and diffused modes of thinking?

### Active/Focus Mode

Active/Focus Mode means when we are assigned a particular task or playing game. We shift out entire focus to that particular task while doing. This type of focus is called Active Focus.

### Diffused Mode

This occurs when our mind is relaxed and we are not concentrating on one particular problem. For suppose if we are working on a paritcular problem and we couldnt able to solve that. Just take a small break and comeback, We might get a new idea to approach the problem.
